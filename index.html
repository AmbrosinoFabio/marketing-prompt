Anatomy of a good prompt

The official prompting guides agree on a few simple habits. Every prompt in the library follows them.

  1. RoleTell the AI who it should be: "You are an SEO consultant…".
  2. ContextGive the facts: product, audience, data. Wrap long material in tags like <data>.
  3. TaskSay exactly what to do, in numbered steps if there are several.
  4. ConstraintsLimits, things to avoid, and "don't invent facts".
  5. Output formatTable, list, length: describe what the answer should look like.
  6. ExamplesOne or two examples of what you like are worth a page of instructions.
